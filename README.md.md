&#x20;**AquaWatch - Water Supply Analysis**



SQL + Power BI project to analyze water shortage, tanker orders, and complaints across Hyderabad areas.



&#x20; **Files**

\- Database\_Creation.sql - Tables creation

\- SQL\_Views - Views for analysis

\- Data\_Quality\_Checks.sql - Data validation

\- Business\_Analysis.sql - Business insights

\- Aqua\_Watch.xlsx - Raw data



&#x20;**Key Findings**

\- 1180 complaints Resolved, 265 In Progress, 155 Open

\- Bachupally has highest tanker expense (Rs 80,041) and 607.8 shortage hours

\- Nagole and Jubilee Hills are also high-risk areas
